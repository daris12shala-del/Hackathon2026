FIND IT — PHOTO STUDIO

HOW IMAGES ARE ARRANGED:
Hero: small cropped photos inside dashboard showcase, 28px chips.
Feature cards: wide 190px visual, cover crop + hover zoom.
Editorial photo sections: large 3-column 250px images.
Story split sections: 430px photo next to text.
Dashboard: square thumbnail images, not stretched emoji replacements.
Mobile: photo cards stack, features show photo before text.

EDIT / REPLACE AN IMAGE:
Find its <img> element by searching its alt text in index.html, how.html, dashboard.html, features.html or about.html.
Change the src URL to another suitable photo URL.
Change object-position in style.css to move the crop.
Change height on .fi-story-frame or .fi-feature-picture to adjust photo space.
Use object-fit:cover to fill without distortion.
Photos load from images.unsplash.com and need internet.
myhome.html is identical to Photo Edition, left untouched.
