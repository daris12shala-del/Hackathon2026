COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

📍 Does FIND IT use GPS?

No. FIND IT remembers locations that you manually register. It does not track the physical position of your belongings.

💾 Do my items remain after refreshing?

Yes, provided your browser supports local storage and you have not cleared the saved data.

🎙️ Can I use the Voice Assistant?

Yes. Open My Home to type commands or use speech recognition when your browser supports it.

🏡 Can I design my own home?

Yes. You can create and manage floors and rooms using the My Home interface.

📦 Can I organize different kinds of items?

Yes. Dashboard provides categories and search tools for your belongings.
