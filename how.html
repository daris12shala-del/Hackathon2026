COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

Does FIND IT use GPS?

No. FIND IT remembers locations you manually register.

Do my items remain after refreshing?

Yes, if local storage is available and has not been cleared.

Can I use Voice Assistant?

Yes. Open My Home and talk to the assistant or type commands.
