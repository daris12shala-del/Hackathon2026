OUR MISSION

Less searching. More living.

FIND IT helps people remember the last saved location of their belongings. Instead of searching every drawer, you can open your collection and see where you recorded the item.

Our project combines item organization, interactive 3D home design and voice commands into one easy-to-use experience.
