THE STORY BEHIND FIND IT

We make finding things simple.

A useful idea for a problem everyone knows: forgetting where we put something.

OUR MISSION

Less searching. More living.

FIND IT helps people remember the last saved location of their belongings.

Instead of searching every drawer, you can open your collection and see where you recorded the item.

Our project also explores 3D home design and voice commands to make finding belongings more intuitive.
